Brand Discovery Document

Tell Us Who
You Really Are.

This questionnaire is the foundation of your brand refresh. Your answers will shape the story we tell, the identity we build, and the perception we engineer — in boardrooms, courtrooms, and beyond.

Law Firm Brand Refresh  ·  Confidential  ·  Strategy & Creative Discovery

How to approach this: There are no wrong answers here. Be direct and honest — the more specific you are, the sharper the work we produce for you. This document covers six areas: your firm's identity, reputation, story, visual direction, name, and aspirations. Set aside 30–45 minutes to complete it fully.

01 Firm Identity & Foundation
Q1
What is the full legal name of your firm, and how long have you been in operation?
Q2
What areas of law do you practice, and which is your primary specialty?
List all practice areas, then indicate which one defines you most.
Q3
Who is your ideal client? Describe them as specifically as possible.
Think beyond demographics — what are they worried about? What do they need from you?
Q4
In one sentence, what does your firm do that no other firm does — or does better?
02 Reputation & Current Perception
Q5
What are you currently known for in the market? What do clients say about you most often?
Q6
If a potential client Googled your firm today and formed an impression, what do you think that impression would be?
Be honest — this gap between reality and perception is often where rebrand work begins.
Q7
What misconception do people most commonly have about your firm — and what's the truth?
Q8
How would you describe your firm's current brand personality?
Q9
Who are your top 2–3 competitors, and how do you see them positioned in the market?
03 The Story You Want to Tell
Q10
Why was this firm founded — and does that origin still drive what you do today?
The founding story is often the most authentic thing a brand owns. Don't skip this.
Q11
If your firm were a person walking into a room, how would they be described?
Think presence, energy, the way they speak. What do they make people feel?
Q12
What do you want to be remembered for, 20 years from now?
Q13
What values are non-negotiable for your firm — the ones that you would never compromise even under commercial pressure?
Q14
Complete this sentence: "We exist so that our clients never have to feel ____."
04 Brands & Firms You Admire
Q15
Name 2–3 law firms — local or international — whose brand or reputation you genuinely admire. What specifically do you admire about each?
It could be their positioning, visual identity, how they communicate, or how they're perceived by clients.
Q16
Name 2–3 brands outside of law — any industry — whose identity, aesthetics, or reputation you find compelling.
Think about how they feel, not just what they look like. What emotion do they evoke?
Q17
Are there any brands or firms — in or out of law — whose identity you actively do NOT want to resemble? Why?
05 Visual Identity & Logo Direction
Q18
How would you describe the visual direction you want to move toward?
Q19
For your logo, what type of mark are you leaning toward?
Q20
Are there any symbols, imagery, or visual motifs that feel meaningful to the firm's identity or history?
Scales of justice, architectural references, initials, geographical symbols — anything you feel connected to, or want to avoid.
Q21
What colours are you drawn to for the new brand, and why? Do any colours feel completely wrong?
Q22
Rate where you want the brand to sit on each of these scales.
Traditional
Contemporary
Corporate & formal
Human & warm
Quiet & understated
Bold & assertive
Local & rooted
International & global
Q23
Where will this brand show up most? Tick everything that applies.
06 The Name
Q24
Is the firm name changing as part of this rebrand?
Q25
If a name change is on the table, what kind of name are you considering?
Q26
If you have any name ideas or directions you're already exploring, list them here — along with what you like about each.
Q27
Are there any naming conventions, words, or structures that are off the table — for legal, cultural, or personal reasons?
07 Goals & Success Criteria
Q28
What is the primary reason you're doing this rebrand right now? What has changed — or needs to change?
Q29
When this rebrand is complete and successful, what will have changed? How will you know it worked?
Q30
Is there anything else — context, concerns, non-negotiables, or hopes — that you want us to know before we begin?

Your responses are confidential and will only be used to shape the creative and strategic direction of your brand refresh.
